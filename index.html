Who we are

A non-profit, non-governmental and non-partisan organisation working across Nigeria.

We teach people about their rights, help settle disputes peacefully, and support women, children, people with disabilities, displaced people and older people.

When someone brings us a complaint, we record it, explain their options and refer them to the right authority, such as the Police, the National Human Rights Commission or legal aid providers.

How we handle a complaint

A simple, respectful process.

  1. Listen and recordWe receive the complaint and record statements given freely.
  2. Explain the optionsWe tell the person what help is available.
  3. Mediate or referWe arrange voluntary mediation, or refer the matter to the Police, NHRC, NAPTIP, courts or legal aid.
