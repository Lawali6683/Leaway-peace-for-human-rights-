About LPFHRA

Leadway Peace Foundation for Human Rights Aid International is a non-profit, non-partisan organisation working for justice, peace and human dignity in Nigeria.

Our vision

A society where every person enjoys dignity, justice, equal protection, peaceful living and the chance to help build their community and country.

Our mission

To protect human rights through public education, lawful advocacy, humanitarian support, voluntary mediation, conflict prevention, community engagement, referrals and partnerships.

Motto: Justice • Peace • Human Rights for All

What we want to achieve

Our main objectives, in simple words.

📘Know your rightsTeach people their rights and responsibilities as citizens.
🗂️Listen and referRecord complaints and send them to the right authority.
🕊️Settle peacefullyVoluntary mediation and dialogue in civil and community disputes.
🛡️Prevent harmStop violence, discrimination, trafficking and child abuse through education and safeguarding.
🤲Support the vulnerableWomen, children, people with disabilities, displaced and older people.
🌱Build communitiesPeace education, youth leadership, health, farming and skills.

Our core values

  1. JusticeFairness and respect for the rule of law.
  2. DignityEquality, inclusion and no discrimination.
  3. PeaceDialogue, patience and non-violence.
  4. IntegrityHonesty, openness and accountability.
  5. ProtectionPrivacy and safety for vulnerable people.
  6. ServiceVolunteering and careful use of resources.

What we do

  • Run public education and training
  • Receive complaints and explain options
  • Arrange voluntary mediation
  • Make community visits and humanitarian support
  • Refer serious cases to the Police, NHRC, NAPTIP, courts or legal aid

What we do not do

  • We are not a court, police body or government agency
  • We are not a diplomatic mission
  • We do not force anyone to attend mediation
  • We do not give unauthorised legal advice
  • We do not claim UN or government links without written proof

Where we work

Our structure runs from the national level down to the community.

  1. NationalHeadquarters in Kano State.
  2. Zonal and stateOffices across Nigeria.
  3. Local and communityChapters close to the people.
